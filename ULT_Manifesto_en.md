#### 1.1.2.The Contingency of History
Many political science theories with widespread influence, such as the [End of History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/End_of_history) and Marxism, hold that the trend of history is inevitable; the debate is just what the end of history should be.  
But I think history is inherently driven by contingency.  
Such as the [Soviet nuclear submarine in the Cuban missile crisis](#a1soviet-nuclear-submarine-in-the-cuban-missile-crisis) incident, a lot of historical trends have been driven by the chance occurrence of rare events such as a few people overturning the majority.  
A more convincing example is that the emergence of human beings from animals is itself a small probability event of evolution, and it is obviously unrealistic to expect animal society to go to the inevitable end of those theories; the fact that Earth became a place capable of sustaining life is an even small probability event.

Of course, I do not deny that in specific historical periods, there may be many high probability regularities, but most of those regularities are effective only during stable development stages. When the development of these regularities approaches its limits, society becomes unstable, giving rise to a multitude of low probability paths—each of which can drive history toward a radically different course. **It is precisely these low probability events that drive history to leap into different periods, thereby manifesting different high probability regularities**. Transitional periods of change are often shorter than periods of stable historical development, which is why it is all too easy to fall into the illusion of inevitable trend.
#### 1.1.3.Strong Countries Bully Weak Countries
From ancient times to the present, strong countries have bullied weak countries, annexing their territory or plundering their interests.

There is an old saying in China that summarizes the historical regularity of a certain  period: *The general trend of the world is that after a long period of division, there will inevitably be unification, and after a long period of c, there will inevitably be division.*  
It means that, given enough time, China’s various historical dynasties would inevitably cycle between periods of unified imperial rule and eras of fragmentation among competing warlords.  
After a long period of division, there will inevitably be unification. This fully illustrates the principle that strong countries bully weak countries.  
After a long period of unification, there will inevitably be division. This implies that no dynasty throughout history has escaped the cycle of history and has ultimately fragmented: This is why it is necessary to do `stability maintenance` after `unity`.  
The reason why the unification is limited to China is that ancient science and technology are not developed enough, and the lack of administrative ability and communication ability makes expansion limited. For example, the Mongolian Empire and Alexander Empire eventually split up, since they took too long for their decrees to spread from the capital to the frontier.

The world outside China is also full of wars between countries, and finally there are several watersheds between war and peace: World War I, World War II, nuclear deterrence system.  
Before World War I, the rulers of various countries considered whether to wage war based solely on `how much benefit could be gained`, `whether they could win` and `how much the war would cost`.  
World War I caused many European monarchs to lose their titles and imposed heavy costs on numerous countries; consequently, the League of Nations was established.  
This led the rulers of various nations to begin, for the first time, to question the legitimacy of war itself, regardless of its causes. Previously, waging war in response to the assassination of a crown prince had always been considered a completely justifiable cause; however, the unprecedented devastation of World War I finally prompted people to reflect on the war itself, rather than merely whether it had a justifiable cause.  
The whole world was relatively peaceful in the following decades. However, due to unavoidable reasons such as `instinctive hunger for interests`,` the victorious countries of World War I bullied Germany in a non-war manner `and` appeasement policies towards the countries that launched the war `, the international order established by World War I was broken.  
World War II broke out, and it was far more devastating than World War I, which led to the emergence of a new international order—the United Nations.  
Leaders around the world are once again learning the lessons of the past—just as they did immediately after World War I—and are carefully reconsidering the peace.  
But even so, the Cold War continued without starting wars, using every means other than war.  
Later, the five permanent members of the UN Security Council and a few other countries acquired nuclear weapons, and there are also unpredictable [dead-hand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Hand) like systems, forming a nuclear deterrent system.  
war against nuclear states and their common defense alliances is often hard or confined within a certain range.  
But the war against non-nuclear states has not yet been curtailed, however that's not the point.

The point is that the peace brought about by the watersheds is temporary and fundamentally unstable.

Only when the central government controls the violent agencies can civil war be avoided to the greatest extent possible, and a situation of regional warlords and military factions fighting among themselves be prevented.  
Countries may maintain their armed forces, police, and other violent agencies, but any routine operations must follow strictly defined top-down rules, and any non-routine deployments must be authorized step by step from the central government down.  
Maintaining countries' military forces helps preserve local stability and prevent the unrest that would result from drastic disarmament or sudden changes in military presence.

Why is it necessary to add the word `human` before `central`? Because we are the human society, we must ensure that the command and grassroots units of violent agencies remain firmly in human hands.

Once the inhuman forces within that violent agencies out of control, the people—and indeed human society as a whole—can easily fall in a situation where they are *at the mercy of others*, forced to confront a more terrifying force with their own flesh and blood.  
This is not to say that non-human entities have absolutely no place within violent agencies, but it must be ensured that they operate solely as fully human-controlled auxiliary tools within grassroots human units, and that at decision-making node, they are strictly used only as a reference within established procedures (strictly avoiding situations where decision-makers are lazy in duties by over-relying on non-human assistance).

Those may also give rise to antisocial and anti-human individuals who don't have any ties and any concern for anything; they would destroy human society without any burden.

As long as there are more people who are determined to oppose society and humanity, the probability that some of them can illegally acquire destructive weapons will increase, and it will become a [sword of Damocles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sword_of_Damocles) hanging over the head of human society.  
So **reduce social conflicts below the risk threshold as much as possible**. In the process of pursuing a happy life, don't forget to bring the bottom people, and the well-being should be attributed to all mankind.

Therefore, in order to protect the fruits of unity, the living environment of all law-abiding people must be guaranteed and social conflicts must be under control.
### 2.2.Methods of Stability Maintenance
#### 2.2.1.Stability Maintenance of Economy
To maintain stability, ensure the living environment of law-abiding people.  
To ensure the living environment of law-abiding people, mainly start with the economy.  
Economic policies are primarily divided into planned economies and market economies.

A small amount of planned economy can prevent special extreme economic risks, but there are many disadvantages when it is dominated by planned economy.  
Modern economy involves many aspects, comprehensive planned economy will invest incalculable administrative costs, the rationality of economic planning is difficult to guarantee.  
There is no way to guarantee that officials in charge of the planned economy will always fulfill their duty to exercise due diligence.  
For example, they might easily be able to determine whether industry or agriculture requires more resource allocation, but expecting them to figure out which—nanoscale silicon micropowder or indium phosphide substrate material—needs more allocation is a bit of a pipe dream. Nor should one expect them to seriously examine non-utilitarian needs like hospice and memorial services for pets that benefit public happiness.  
They will feel that their efforts are mentally and emotionally draining without yielding any tangible results; instead, arbitrarily distributing benefits or lining their own pockets is more in line with their profit-seeking and harm-avoiding instinct.  
If the comprehensive planned economy can still maintain the good operation of society, it shows that the productive forces of society far exceed the various needs of the people.  
But in this case, the market economy can also maintain the reasonable needs of ordinary people.

In contrast, the administrative costs required for a market economy are very low.  
Resources automatically flow to where they are most needed, where they are willing to pay the highest price.  
Prices that are too high or too low tend to balance because of supply and demand, and the enthusiasm for wealth creation will be mobilized.  
It is an economic policy that is easy to implement and extremely effective; [reform and opening up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_and_opening_up) of China is an excellent proof.  
However, a market economy inherently leads to the [Matthew Effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_effect), those who control more resources gain an advantage in production, causing the gap between the rich and the poor to widen.  
Especially finance, unlike other industries that rely on consumption of outputs to earn income, it earns income through a proportion of principal. Ordinary people's income is not better than the interest on the savings of the rich.  
Only measure returns from finance in decibels can place them on the same graph as the absolute returns from other industries.  
However the gap between the rich and the poor is too large, which will make most people unable to consume, thus making it difficult for the economy to function normally.  
Therefore, in order to protect the living environment of law-abiding people and avoid the risk of internal collapse brought about by economic crisis, we must not completely allow the market economy to develop in disorder.  
It is necessary to make the income of the people at the bottom quite a bit higher than that needed for the normal operation of the economy, so as to maintain and promote the development of the economy and bring the whole mankind closer to a better and happy life.  
To teach the rich that they have to make everyone has money for to spend for staying rich. Ford doubled workers' incomes, greatly boosted the economy and made it more profitable.  
**The united unificated country should view money as a tool for harnessing those who are good at creating wealth and imbuing them with enthusiasm for creating better living conditions for all mankind.**

Measures are also needed to prevent the wealth gap from exceeding an unacceptable level.  
For example, collecting the hierarchical asset taxes. After the assets reach a certain level, it is difficult to cover the asset tax through financial income, so that the wealth of the rich will not be endless and unlimited.  
However, the hierarchy must be structured reasonably. The tax rate for the modest rich should be very low which is better than nothing, while the tax rate for the extremely rich should ensure that three generations of their family can be rich and carefree even without any income.  
This ensures that money is attractive enough to entice capable people to create wealth for all mankind. The purpose of the hierarchical asset taxes is not to prevent people from becoming rich, but to control the upper limit of richness.  
This tax and other taxes can be used for secondary distribution to limit the gap between rich and poor, as well as for projects that capital generally does not want to touch, such as long-term investment projects with long payback periods and projects that yield benefits more important than financial gain.  
This method is just one of my ideas, just a reference rather than a dogma, and I would be very happy if the rulers of the united unificated country had a better way.
#### 2.2.2.No Grey Laws and Policies
Policies, laws and regulations must be strictly enforced; not to apply selectively and dishearten the people, to avoid falling into the dangerous [Tacitus Trap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacitus_Trap).  
If an ambiguous gray industry, or a black industry which goes unpunished due to selective law enforcement, earns far more than a white industry, compliance is a huge loss.  
The more pronounced this situation is, the greater the damage it causes to the rules; however strictly someone abides by the law, it is difficult *to remain unstained despite emerging from the mire*—the raging tide of the environment will sweep almost everyone off course.  
For example, if by evading customs duties smugglers can easily earn in just a few months what law-abiding merchants earn over several years—and face no punishment or only a token penalty—then more and more people will turn to smuggling, making customs regulations losses for law-abiding merchants on the contrary. Merchants may be able to stick to the rules for a year or even several years, but what about ten years down the road? Almost no one can resist this temptation.  
And illegal immigrants in the United States can get far more privileges from a Democratic government than legal immigrants and legal citizens, so compliance with the rules will be a huge loss to legal immigrants, undermine the credibility of immigration and citizenship laws.  
And China's rampant marriage fraud problem, even if punished, only a little more than half of the bride price will be returned, only a few months can harvest tens of thousands or even hundreds of thousands, if this continues, then however good and principled a girl is, it will be difficult to resist such temptation, and finally the sanctity and credibility of marriage will disappear.

### A.1.Soviet Nuclear Submarine in the Cuban Missile Crisis
During the Cuban Missile Crisis in the Cold War, the United States and the Soviet Union began a high-risk military confrontation.  
During that time, a Soviet nuclear submarine was surrounded by a U.S. fleet and forced to surface.  
There are several very high risk points in the process as follows:
1. The United States used depth charges to drive away the nuclear submarine; at the time, the sole application of this kind of depth charges was to drive away and force to surface, but the Soviet side was unaware of this, and the crew on board the submarine believed it was an aggressive move by the U.S. fleet.
2. There was a rule on Soviet nuclear submarines which is `If the submarine comes under attack and cannot establish contact with Moscow, the captain, first officer and political commissar may authorize the launch of nuclear weapons by unanimous decision.`. However, while all three did believe at the time that the ship had come under attack, one of them went against the consensus and vetoed the proposal to launch a nuclear weapon.
3. In the end, the Soviet nuclear submarine, unable to fight back, was forced to surface and prepare to accept the capture of the United States; however, the United States ultimately did not capture it but let it go.
4. Although the three decision-makers aboard the nuclear submarine prevented a nuclear war that would have destroyed mankind, the more than 80 crew members who eventually returned to the Soviet Union were all discharged from the military, and the first officer ultimately chose to take his own life. demonstrating that the Soviet Union’s internal policy toward surrender and compromise was one of severe repression, and that decisions made by the leadership would result in collective punishment of ordinary crew members who had no decision-making authority. Under such circumstances, the decision-maker's decision will be involuntary in many cases, and even reasonable decisions may be overturned from the bottom up.

It can be seen that the result of no nuclear war breaking out in the end is the result of the multiplication of many small probabilities. Perhaps in most parallel universes in quantum mechanics, mankind has long been destroyed by nuclear war.
